Privacy Policy

Color Tap — Fast Reflex Game

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arovyx Games ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Color Tap mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary: We collect minimal data needed to run the game — your scores, gameplay analytics, and (if you register) your account info. We do not sell your personal data. Game scores you submit to the leaderboard are visible to other players by design.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: If you register, we collect your email address, display name, and profile photo URL (when you sign in with Google or email).
  • Profile data: Avatar selection, display name, and country preference stored in your profile.
  • Gameplay data: Scores, difficulty played, correct/wrong answers, max combo, game duration, and session timestamps.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Analytics data: App opens, screen views, game start/end events, feature interactions, and performance metrics via Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash reports: Stack traces, device state, and app version via Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose and fix bugs.
  • Advertising ID: Your device's advertising identifier (GAID/IDFA) is collected by our ad partners for ad personalisation and measurement.
  • Device information: OS version, device model, app version, language, and timezone.
  • Network information: IP address and connection type for network requests and offline sync.

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In: Google provides us with your basic profile (name, email, photo) when you choose to sign in.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store: Purchase history and subscription status for Plus/Pro tiers.
  • Ad networks (AdMob, Unity, Meta, AppLovin, ironSource, InMobi, Start.io): These partners may collect data as described in their own privacy policies.

3. Data Storage

3.1 Local Storage

Game scores, settings, and profile data are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences and Hive. This data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the App.

3.2 Cloud Storage

When you register an account, your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (asia-south1 region) including:

  • User profile (display name, email, photo URL, preferences)
  • Game score records (score, difficulty, combo, duration, timestamp)
  • Leaderboard entries (weekly best scores per difficulty and country)
  • Username reservations (unique permanent usernames)

3.3 Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained while your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: When you delete your account, we remove your profile, scores, and leaderboard entries within 30 days.
  • Analytics data: Retained per Firebase's default retention (up to 14 months for event data).
  • Crash reports: Retained for 90 days.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in these circumstances:

  • Leaderboard visibility: When you submit a score, your display name, score, difficulty, and country are visible to other players on the global and friends leaderboards.
  • Service providers: Google (Firebase, Play Billing, Sign-In), Apple (App Store, StoreKit), and ad network partners process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred to the new owner.

5. Advertising

The App displays third-party advertisements through a unified ad waterfall including AdMob, Unity, Meta, AppLovin, ironSource, InMobi, and Start.io. These partners may use your advertising ID and device information to serve personalised ads.

  • Banner ads: Displayed on Home, Difficulty, Game Over, Leaderboard, Settings, and Profile screens.
  • Interstitial ads: Full-screen ads shown after every 3rd game over and during screen transitions (with a 3-minute cooldown).
  • Rewarded ads: Optional ads you can watch to continue after game over, get a 2x score boost, or claim a daily bonus.

If you subscribe to Color Tap Plus or Color Tap Pro, all banner and interstitial ads are removed. Rewarded ads remain available as they are optional and provide in-game benefits.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate data via your in-app profile.
  • Deletion: Delete your account from the Profile screen, or contact us for assistance.
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Opt-out of ads: You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings or use the "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" setting on Android/iOS.
  • Withdraw consent: You can stop using the App at any time and delete your account.

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmission uses TLS encryption.
  • Firestore security rules restrict access to user data (owner-only writes, registered-only leaderboard).
  • Firebase Authentication handles credential storage with industry-standard hashing.
  • Score validation rules prevent impossible scores from being uploaded.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information.

9. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to external websites (e.g., Play Store rating page, social sharing). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of these third-party services.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via an in-app notice or by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
