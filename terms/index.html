1. Description of Service

Color Tap is a fast-paced Stroop-effect reflex game where players tap the colour circle matching the meaning of a displayed word (not its ink colour) before a timer runs out. The App includes:

  • Core gameplay with Easy, Medium, Hard, and Hard+ (Pro) difficulty modes
  • Combo scoring system and speed progression
  • Global and friends leaderboards with weekly rankings
  • Achievement badges and profile customisation
  • Festival themes activated remotely during holiday seasons
  • Offline play with cloud sync when connectivity is restored
  • Optional subscriptions: Color Tap Plus and Color Tap Pro
  • Third-party advertisements (removable via subscription)
